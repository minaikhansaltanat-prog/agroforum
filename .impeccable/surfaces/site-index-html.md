## Scope and visitor mode

Persuade. The single-page Agroforum 2026 landing page (`site/index.html`), five languages (Kazakh default, Russian, Chinese, Uzbek, Kyrgyz), phone first.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

Working farmers and farm or agrofirm heads from four regions decide on a phone; partner companies want ready buyers. The action is to reserve a seat (Participant 5 000 ₸, Dinner 30 000 ₸) or to become a partner (Exhibition 100 000 ₸, Speaker 150 000 ₸). Proof on hand: the real programme, five KazNARU scientists with talk titles, four speaker videos, three organiser logos, real photographs. No invented testimonials, statistics or logos. Constraints from TZ.md: light cream design, Montserrat and Inter self-hosted, no Google services, only transform and opacity animation, 360 px first, accessibility AA.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The forum's own title is a dialogue between science and practice, so the page is one conversation, the farmer's question and the scientist's answer, and refuses the conference-brochure arrangement of hero, logo row, speaker grid and price table.

OWN-WORLD: Cream ground (#FBF8F1) with fine grain, white cards at 20 to 28 px radius with navy-tinted soft shadows, navy ink (#0B1F3A), gold (#C9A24A) for actions and a flat sun disc, green (#2E7D4F) for answers and ticks; Montserrat 800 headings with tight tracking, Inter text; the packages are seat passes with perforated notches; questions sit in white or sand bubbles, answers in green-washed bubbles; real steppe, field, herd and laboratory photography in rounded frames.

STORY: A farmer finds their own question in the list, sees it answered, sees the price and that the hall holds 250 people, and reserves a seat; a supplier sees ready buyers and 20 places and applies.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: date and place chips, the full forum title (navy lines, then the green line "Ғылым мен тәжірибе диалогы" with a gold bar), one sentence promising three outcomes, a gold "register as participant" button and a white "become a partner" button, a price and seat note, a live countdown. Right column: a three-photograph collage (wheat field, scientist, herd) over a gold sun disc, with one question bubble and one answer bubble overlapping the photos.

FORM: TZ.md section 3 pins the visual world (light editorial, palette, fonts, rounded cards, soft shadows), so the dice only shaped structure: it assigned index 7 of my grounded list (seed:2520abf36d4b8bb8), which is the two-voice dialogue (question and answer), and that is what was built.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
